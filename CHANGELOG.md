# Changelog

## 0.1.1 — 2026-11-03

- One window button: minimize now remembers the exact position and reopens from the Quota pill there.
- The Quota pill shows the monthly percentage.
- Fixed reopening after closing landing at a different position.
- Removed the extra icon next to Forecast.

## 0.1.0 — 2026-11-03

- First release.
- Floating OpenCode Go quota card with 5-hour, weekly and monthly meters.
- Monthly forecast with an expand animation and the Console usage chart style.
- Draggable, dockable card that stays on screen and opens upward near the bottom.
- Titlebar pill, `mod+shift+q`, command palette actions, and a Quota launcher after close.
- Reads the quota through the OpenCode Desktop server; no credentials or separate network calls.
